Vocantly is a chat and customer-support platform you embed in your own product.
Add **real-time messaging**, a **support widget with an AI bot**, and **voice/video calls** —
using a headless JavaScript SDK, a drop-in widget, or the REST + WebSocket APIs directly.

## How it fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an app">
    In the dashboard, create an **App** to get an **App ID**, a **public key** (`pk_…`),
    and a **secret key** (`sk_…`). Keep the secret on your backend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint a token on your backend">
    Your backend calls the API to issue a short-lived **SDK token** for the current user.
    The frontend receives only the token — never your keys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect from the frontend">
    The SDK (or widget) connects to the real-time service with that token and streams
    messages, presence, typing, receipts, and call signaling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Prefer a no-backend embed? A static site can open an anonymous support session with just
  your **public key** and an allow-listed origin — see [Authentication & tokens](/guides/authentication).
</Note>
